Privacy Policy

Compliant with India's DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR • Effective: November 24, 2026

1. Overview & Data Roles

This Privacy Policy explains how CheckinOS Technologies Pvt. Ltd. processes information. In the context of hospitality data (hotel guests, check-in records, passport/ID numbers, folio balances):

  • Your Hotel is the Data Fiduciary (under India DPDP Act) or Data Controller (under GDPR).
  • CheckinOS acts strictly as a Data Processor performing computing operations solely on your instruction.

2. Data We Collect & Store

We process the following categories of data on behalf of our subscriber hotels:

  • Staff Account Information: Name, work email address, role, and hashed authentication credentials.
  • Guest Profiles: Full name, contact telephone, email address, nationality, government ID references, and room preferences.
  • Transaction & Folio Records: Nightly rates, incidental charges, tax breakdowns, and payment confirmation references (we never store raw credit card numbers or CVVs).
  • Operational Metadata: Audit trails, timestamped front desk status modifications, and housekeeping assignments.

3. Guest Rights & Data Subject Requests

Under the DPDP Act and GDPR, hotel guests have the right to access their personal records, request corrections of erroneous data, or request erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"). CheckinOS provides immediate built-in tools for hotel managers to soft-delete or permanently purge guest profile records in accordance with statutory requirements.

4. Data Retention & Safeguards

All data is maintained within secure, encrypted cloud data centers in Mumbai, India. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or train machine learning models on any customer or guest information. Data is retained only for the active lifecycle of your hotel subscription or as required by applicable tax legislation.

5. Data Protection Officer (DPO) Contact

For any privacy inquiries, audits, or regulatory notices, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@checkinos.io.
