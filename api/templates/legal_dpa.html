Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Standard Contractual Clauses • Effective: November 24, 2026

1. Scope and Applicability

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") supplements the CheckinOS Terms of Service and governs the processing of personal data related to your property's guests, visitors, and personnel. This agreement satisfies statutory requirements under Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of India.

2. Processing Instructions

CheckinOS shall process Personal Data exclusively on documented instructions from the Customer (the Controller), including regarding transfers of Personal Data to third countries or international organizations, unless required to do so by applicable statutory law.

3. Technical & Organizational Measures (TOMs)

CheckinOS maintains rigorous technical safeguards to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:

  • End-to-end cryptographic transit encryption using TLS 1.3.
  • AES-256 block-level data encryption at rest for database clusters and automated backup images.
  • Role-based access controls limiting internal system access to authorized engineering staff via multi-factor authentication (MFA).
  • Continuous vulnerability scanning and automated daily integrity verification.

4. Sub-Processor Engagement

Customer grants CheckinOS general authorization to engage vetted third-party sub-processors (including Amazon Web Services, Stripe, and SendGrid) to deliver hosting, payment gateway, and transactional communication infrastructure. CheckinOS remains fully liable for the performance of each sub-processor's data protection obligations.

5. Incident Notification SLA

In the unlikely event of a confirmed security incident impacting customer data, CheckinOS will notify the affected Customer's primary administrative contact within 48 hours of confirmation, providing detailed remediation steps and impact analysis.
