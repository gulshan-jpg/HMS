Frequently Asked Questions

How does the hotel demo and setup work?
Our product specialists schedule a 20-minute live demo configured to your property type, room count, and operational flow. We demonstrate real-time check-ins, housekeeping dispatch, multi-folio billing, GST invoicing, and data import from your existing software.
Can we import our existing guests and rooms from our old PMS?
Yes. CheckinOS includes a built-in CSV bulk import wizard with pre-validation. Download our sample CSV templates for guests and rooms, paste your data, and import hundreds of rows in under 10 seconds.
Do we need specialized hardware or local servers?
No hardware required whatsoever. CheckinOS is 100% cloud-native and works inside any modern browser on PC, Mac, iPad, Android tablets, or mobile phones. Front desk computers require only a modern web browser.
Is CheckinOS compliant with Indian GST requirements?
Yes. Our invoice engine automatically handles CGST, SGST, and IGST breakdowns according to Indian tax regulations. Invoices include GSTIN, SAC codes, and sequential fiscal year numbering.
What if our internet connection drops at the front desk?
CheckinOS includes an offline-tolerant service worker that caches the current room rack, arrival list, and checked-in guest folios. Front desk staff can continue looking up room assignments and guest details even during temporary network interruptions.
How does the mobile housekeeping view work?
Managers can generate a secure 24-hour signed link from the dashboard and share it via WhatsApp or SMS to cleaning staff. Housekeeping opens the link without needing an email or password, sees their floor's rooms, and marks rooms clean with one tap.
What payment methods do you accept for subscriptions?
We process subscriptions securely through Stripe. We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, UPI, and net banking. Tax invoices with your hotel's GST number are automatically emailed upon renewal.
